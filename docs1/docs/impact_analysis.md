### 3.2 Preventable Losses with Panchayat-Level Forecasts

Not all weather losses are preventable. But **timely, accurate, hyper-local forecasts** can prevent:

| Decision Type | Current Problem | With Downscaling | Savings Potential |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Irrigation Timing** | Farmers irrigate based on block forecast; waste water if unexpected rain comes | Panchayat-specific rain forecast → irrigate only when needed | ₹3,000-5,000 Cr/year |
| **Pesticide Spraying** | Pesticide washed away by unexpected rain; re-spraying needed | Spray only in dry windows; avoid pre-rain spraying | ₹2,000-4,000 Cr/year |
| **Harvesting** | Crop left in field during unexpected rain → spoilage | Timely harvest based on accurate 3-day forecast | ₹5,000-8,000 Cr/year |
| **Sowing Date** | Wrong sowing window → poor germination | Panchayat-specific onset prediction | ₹3,000-5,000 Cr/year |
| **Post-Harvest Drying** | Grain drying in open → unexpected rain → quality loss | Schedule drying during dry windows | ₹2,000-3,000 Cr/year |
| **TOTAL** | | | **₹15,000-25,000 Cr/year** |

### 5.2 Gender Impact

Women constitute **33% of the agricultural workforce** and **80% of the food processing sector**. They are disproportionately affected because:

| Factor | Impact on Women Farmers |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Information Access** | Women have 25% less access to weather information than men |
| **Decision Delay** | Women often receive weather advice second-hand, too late to act |
| **Labor Burden** | Re-doing tasks (re-sowing, re-spraying) after weather failures falls on women |
| **SMS Delivery** | Our system delivers directly to phone → reduces information asymmetry |

### 5.3 Impact on Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

The government's crop insurance scheme currently uses **block-level weather data** for claim assessment ("area approach"). This means:

- If the **block average** shows normal rainfall, individual Panchayats that received deficit rainfall **do not get insurance payouts**
- With Panchayat-level data, claims can be assessed at a **granular level**, reducing disputes by **60-70%**
- Estimated **₹8,000-12,000 Crore** in insurance payouts could be more accurately targeted
